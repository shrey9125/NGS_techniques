# Compact glossary

- **Adapter:** synthetic sequence attached during library preparation for sequencing and/or indexing.
- **Alignment:** placement of reads against a reference.
- **Assembly:** reconstruction of longer sequences from overlapping reads.
- **Base quality (Q):** logarithmic estimate of the probability that a called base is wrong; Q30 corresponds to an estimated 1-in-1000 error probability.
- **Coverage/depth:** number of reads or molecules supporting a genomic position.
- **Duplicate:** repeated observation that may come from PCR rather than an independent original molecule.
- **FASTA:** sequence records without per-base qualities.
- **FASTQ:** sequence records with per-base quality characters.
- **Index/barcode:** sequence used to identify a sample, cell, or molecule.
- **Library:** collection of molecules prepared for sequencing.
- **Mapping quality:** confidence that a read was placed at the correct reference location.
- **Paired-end:** sequencing both ends of a library fragment.
- **Phasing:** determining which variants occur on the same chromosome copy.
- **Read:** base sequence inferred from one sequenced molecule or cluster.
- **Reference bias:** preferential alignment or measurement of alleles resembling the reference.
- **UMI:** unique molecular identifier used to recognize original molecules and suppress duplicate/error effects.
- **Variant allele fraction (VAF):** fraction of observations supporting a non-reference allele at a position.
